# PROJECT OVERVIEW: Robot Brick Breaker

## Core Concept
A brick breaker game where instead of a simple platform, the player controls a robot. The robot is equipped with a weapon and can attack the ball in two directions (or more, depending on the weapon). The player can equip different weapons that they craft based on materials collected by breaking bricks throughout the game.

## Material & Crafting System

### Material Collection
- Players obtain materials by breaking bricks during gameplay
- Each biome features unique brick types with specific colors
- Each material is associated with a specific brick color and biome
- Different bricks drop different materials when destroyed
- Players must play specific biomes to collect the materials needed for particular gear or weapon upgrades
- At the end of each stage, the player receives a treasure chest containing money and additional materials

### Weapon Crafting
- Materials are used to craft new weapons and items to be used in stages
- Weapons have durability and break after a certain amount of use
- This encourages a loop of collecting materials and crafting weapons

### Upgrade System
- Materials are also used for permanent upgrades, combined with materials dropped by stage bosses
- Some rare equipment requires multiple boss items to be crafted, encouraging boss farming

## Gameplay Loop

### Map Structure
- When selecting a node on the map, it will have 3 stages plus 1 Boss
- Bosses take extra damage from weapons made with materials dropped in that stage
- The loop: grind on maps, defeat bricks until you have enough gold and materials to craft weapons and items to face the Boss

### Special Mechanics Ideas
- The player is always a robot
- As they hit the ball, they fill up a special bar
- Once the bar is filled, the player can unleash a special power related to their equipped core
- Each core could have a special ultimate skill unique to that equipped core

### Parry System
- Parry occurs when the player hits the ball in a specific frame
- This time window can be increased with skill upgrades
- Skill tree can be upgraded to add damage and effects to parry

### Slow Motion Zone
- A zone below the player where the ball enters slow motion
- Gives the player an opportunity to recover the ball before being punished
- Or the player can unlock skills to speed up or slow down the game speed to make stages more dynamic

### Launch Skill
- At the beginning of a stage, the player can repeatedly press the button to give more power to the ball
- Can be either tap or hold the button

## Robot Customization
- Can the player build their robot?
  - Choose a simple platform?
  - Or a robot with arms?

## Alternative World Structure

### Continuous Map Concept
The game is a large continuous map divided into segments:
- Each segment has a boss at the end followed by a checkpoint
- Player has freedom to explore this area as they wish within established limits
- Does resting at a checkpoint respawn enemies?

### Biome System
- Segments are biomes made of unique brick types
- Each biome has its own set of bricks that drop biome-specific materials when broken
- Materials are used to create chips or equipment to make the player stronger
- Players must strategically choose which biomes to play based on what materials they need for their desired upgrades
- The player needs to carve a path through this segment made of blocks
- They choose which path to follow, influenced by their crafting and upgrade goals

## Session Time Options
Add an option for session duration - the player can choose how long they want a session to last:
- 3 minutes

- 10 minutes

## Visual Elements

- **First Diagram:** Shows vertical colored rectangles (blue, green, yellow, red, gray) representing different biomes with flame icons between them
- **Second Diagram:** Shows the same colored blocks with arrows indicating a winding path that the player can follow through the biomes, illustrating the freedom of exploration mentioned in the text
